Sounding 01 · two currents, one confluence

Tigris

Tech Labs

An AI-native product holding company, named for the plain that two rivers built. Tigris Tech Labs builds products that go deep into single industries. This is a sounding through it — one continuous section, no pages. There is only how far down you are willing to go.

↓ Scroll, drag, or arrow to descend
0320 m — silt

Everything at the surface looks the same.

A general model, a thin wrapper, a demo that survives exactly as long as the meeting. Loose silt — nothing here has been under pressure long enough to hold anything up.

0640 m — the name

Two rivers met here, and somebody had to start keeping count.

The Tigris and the Euphrates made this plain fertile enough to be worth arguing over. Out of the argument came the first writing — not poetry: counts of grain, terms of trade, obligations that outlived the people who made them.

Accounting, contracts and written law were all cut into wet clay within sight of this water, and are still legible four thousand years later. That is the standard a record is held to.

Three wedges, cut into the disc of the valley — the mark
1200 m — the company

An AI-native product holding company.

We build AI platforms. Each one begins inside a single industry's real constraints — its data, its language, its liability — and stays there until it is indispensable to the people doing the work.

We do not enter a second industry to grow. We go further into the first one.

1580 m — constraint I

Its data

Not a benchmark set. The messy, partial, contradictory record an industry actually keeps — and the reasons it keeps it that way.

1820 m — constraint II

Its language

Every trade has a private vocabulary where one word carries a decade of precedent. Get it wrong and the product is instantly recognisable as an outsider.

2060 m — constraint III

Its liability

Who signs. Who is exposed when the answer is wrong. This is the constraint that decides whether software is adopted or merely admired.

Depth is the strategy.

Breadth is easy to copy. A decade inside one industry's constraints is not. Below this line the silt turns to tablet.

3620 m — philosophy

What a holding company is for.

01 — time

We hold. No forced exit clock, so a product can spend the years it takes to become load-bearing.

02 — spine

Shared infrastructure, evaluation and security. The depth stays local; the machinery is common.

03 — refusal

Most opportunities are declined. A canal that wanders waters nothing.

Canal 01 · cut at 3060 m · peptide research

Cortex

A reasoning layer for peptide research. Cortex renders a whole protocol as one living surface — evidence is brightness, adherence is density, and a conflict shows up as tension you can see before you can name it.

Data

92 compounds across 12 goal categories, each carrying its own evidence grade and cardiovascular rating.

Language

Four depths instead of a menu. You do not navigate pages — you zoom, and zooming out is back.

Liability

Educational reference for adults 18+, quoted verbatim. Where the record is unestablished, Cortex says so instead of paraphrasing.

Canal 03 · cut at 3200 m · commercial insurance

PRAIX

The intelligence engine for commercial insurance. We don't help you send more email — we make you worth meeting. Agents deploy, signals travel, a portfolio assembles, and risk reveals.

Data

A property portfolio assembled from scattered record — loss runs, statements of value, and the flood and wind exposure under every address.

Language

SOV, TIV, x-date, hard market. The vocabulary a producer is judged by on the first call.

Liability

Invitation only, one book at a time. The agents do the intelligence work; the producer gets the action plan and signs it.
